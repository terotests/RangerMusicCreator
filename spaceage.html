Ranger Space Age

The hi-fi lounge music of 1955–1965: exotica, science-fiction theremin, cha-cha "bachelor pad" records, bossa lounge and space waltzes. Pick a style and the composer writes an original tune with an A theme and a B bridge, and arranges it: an intro, the theme, the bridge, an improvised solo on vibes, marimba, organ or theremin, the theme again with more instruments and often a key change, and an outro. Then it writes the next one.

Classics are public-domain melodies: Greensleeves (traditional) as a space waltz. Only public-domain compositions are used; no recording or arrangement by anyone else.

Apollo is music around Apollo 11 (July 1969) and Apollo 13 (April 1970). The songs people played then, and every recording, are still under copyright, so it uses the public-domain works tied to the missions, written out from the free scores: the New World Symphony Largo (Neil Armstrong took a tape of the symphony on Apollo 11), Also sprach Zarathustra (the Apollo 13 crew opened their TV broadcast with it) and The Blue Danube (the docking waltz of the 1968 film). Four more tunes are originals in the 1969 style, named after moments of the missions: Countdown, Tranquility Base, Aquarius Lifeboat and Splashdown. MOON SHOT 1969 remixes these themes with a Moog lead over a 1969 beat, strings, organ, muted brass and radio beeps.

The sounds. Strings, flute, harp, glockenspiel, timpani, muted trumpet and trombone, upright piano, pizzicato bass, marimba, bongos, congas, claves, guiro and maracas are samples from the VS Chamber Orchestra Community Edition by Versilian Studios (CC0, public domain). The vibraphone, the organ, the theremin, the Moog, the radio beeps, the kick drum, the flying saucers and the birds are synthesized in your browser. What you save is yours to use, in games too, with no royalties.
