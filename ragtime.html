Ranger Ragtime

Ragtime and early jazz, 1897–1925. Pick a style and the composer writes an original rag in the classic form: an introduction, a first strain, a second strain, the first strain again, and a trio in a new key, each strain played twice. The piano plays it as a rag is written, with a syncopated right hand over a striding left hand, and the band joins on the repeats: cornet (or xylophone) on the tune, clarinet runs above it, a tailgate trombone, tuba on the two beats and drums.

Joplin plays five of Scott Joplin's rags note for note from the Mutopia Project's public-domain editions. Joplin died in 1917, so his music is free to use everywhere. The band arrangement is made here.

The sounds are real instruments recorded note by note: the VS Chamber Orchestra Community Edition by Versilian Studios, released as CC0 (public domain), including its upright piano, a 1950s trombone, muted trumpet, clarinet, temple blocks and xylophone. Only the hi-hat is synthesized. What you save is yours to use, in games too, with no royalties.

  • BAND: AUTO lets the band join on the repeats; FULL BAND or SOLO PIANO fixes it.
  • BIG FINISH! jumps to the trio with the whole band, ENDING plays the ending and stops.
  • Keys: space play/stop, N new rag, → next strain, C big finish, E ending.
