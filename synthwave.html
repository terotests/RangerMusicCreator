Ranger Synthwave

The sound of 1980s synth-pop and film scores, made new: outrun night drives, Italo disco, darksynth, dreamwave and a TV-show fanfare. Pick a style and the composer writes an original tune with an A theme and a B bridge, and arranges it: an intro, the theme, the bridge, a lead solo, the theme again with brass and choir, often a key change, and an outro. Then it writes the next one.

Classics are public-domain pieces given the synth treatment: Grieg's In the Hall of the Mountain King (1875), getting faster with every round, and the opening of the Toccata in D minor, BWV 565, attributed to J. S. Bach. Only public-domain compositions are used; no recording or arrangement by anyone else.

The sounds are all synthesized in your browser from oscillators and noise: saw leads with echoes, arpeggiators, pads with chorus, an FM electric piano, synth brass, a vowel choir and a drum machine with a gated snare. No samples, so nothing anyone else owns. What you save is yours to use, in games too, with no royalties.

  • NEXT ▸ skips to the next part; OVERDRIVE! jumps to the big finish; ENDING plays the outro.
  • The faders set each section; M mutes it.
  • Keys: space play/stop, N new tune, → next part, C big finish, E ending.
