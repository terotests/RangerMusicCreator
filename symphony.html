    Ranger Symphony

    An orchestra that composes film music for your scenes. Pick a scene and it writes an original theme and orchestrates it through a cinematic form: an intro swell, the theme on a solo instrument, the theme in full strings, a bridge that builds, the climax in the brass a step higher, and a big ending. Then it writes the next theme. The themes are written by rules from a seed, in the manner of large film scores; they are not copies of any film's music.

    Classics are public-domain works (the composers died more than 70 years ago), arranged here and played with the same orchestra. No recordings are used, so nothing in what you save belongs to a record label. Sibelius is left out until his works become free in Finland on 1 January 2028.

    The orchestra is the VS Chamber Orchestra Community Edition sample library by Versilian Studios, released as CC0 (public domain): real players recorded note by note. The hall is a reverb generated in your browser. What you record is yours to use, in games too, with no royalties.

    • INTENSITY sets how big it gets; AUTO SHAPE lets the form build and relax by itself.
    • CLIMAX! jumps to the climax, ENDING plays the ending and stops, NEW THEME writes a new one.
    • The faders under the desk set each section; M mutes it and the melody moves to another section.
    • SAVE WAV renders the piece faster than real time. LINK copies a link to the scene and its seed.
    • Keys: space play/stop, N new theme, → next section, C climax, E ending.
