Ranger Big Band

A swing-era dance band, 1935–1945. Pick a style and the composer writes an original tune and arranges it the way the big bands did: an intro, the tune with the reeds in close harmony and the brass punching, improvised solo choruses with backgrounds from the sections, a shout chorus with the trumpets screaming on top, the tune again and a tag ending. Then it writes the next tune.

Standards are public-domain tunes: When the Saints Go Marching In (traditional) and Scott Joplin's Maple Leaf Rag played as swing, as the swing bands did. Only public-domain compositions are used; no recording or arrangement by anyone else.

The band is sampled from the VS Chamber Orchestra Community Edition by Versilian Studios (CC0, public domain): trumpets open and muted, trombones with falls, clarinets, upright piano, pizzicato bass and drums. The library has no saxophones, so the reed section is clarinets with a bassoon below, the way some swing bands voiced their reeds. The hi-hat is synthesized. What you save is yours to use, in games too, with no royalties.

  • SWING sets the feel; SHOUT! jumps to the shout chorus; ENDING takes it out.
  • The faders set each section; M mutes it.
  • Keys: space play/stop, N new tune, → next part, C shout chorus, E ending.
